Auditing Class Unlearning in ResNet-18 Beyond Output Accuracy

[CIFAR-10 Repo] [MNIST Repo]
Independent Research • PyTorch, scikit-learn (2026)

Most approximate unlearning methods easily achieve success in output space: high accuracy on the retain set (Dr) and near-0% accuracy on the forget set (Df). Scaling my earlier 4-layer CNN (forgetting digit 7 in MNIST) audit up to an 11.17M parameter ResNet-18 (forgetting class 3 (cat) in CIFAR-10), I designed a three-stage audit comparing Gradient Ascent, Bad Teacher Distillation, and Diagonal Fisher Information Pruning against a fully retrained model (oracle). I deliberately did not use MIA here, as with a whole class as the forget set, the retrained oracle treats train and test cats identically, so an MIA collapses into "cat vs. not cat" rather than membership.

1. Controlling for the "BatchNorm Illusion" (Kalani et al., 2026): In residual networks, BatchNorm2d layers retain running mean/variance statistics from original training or get skewed by forget-heavy batches. Before evaluating any checkpoint, I implemented exact retain-set recalibration (resetting stats and computing cumulative moving averages with momentum=None over Dr). Recalibration alone dropped Bad Teacher's train forget accuracy from 8.20% → 2.02% without changing a single weight.

2. Why Output Suppression Increases Latent Separability: Training a balanced logistic regression probe on frozen 512-dimensional penultimate features showed that Bad Teacher actually makes forgotten cat embeddings more linearly separable than the original model (93.96% vs. 92.52% balanced accuracy). This is most likely because Bad Teacher forces uniform logits (∼0.10) on every forget sample while keeping retain predictions peaked. So, the forgotten images cluster into a distinct region of feature space, allowing a 300-step fine-tuning attack to recover 75% accuracy in just 120–150 steps (ReAUC: 0.78–0.85).

3. Hard Masking vs. Synaptic Reset in Fisher Pruning: Using the diagonal Fisher ratio Si = Fii(Df) / Fii(Dr), I zeroed the top 50% (5.25M) forget-specialized weights in layer3, layer4, and residual downsample shortcuts while leaving early layers intact. Comparing locked masks (Hard Pruning) against unlocked healing (Synaptic Reset) showed that allowing retain gradients to overwrite zeroed weights during 2 healing epochs matched the oracle's latent probe (90.10% vs. 90.11%), restored natural semantic confusion (56.6% of cats predicted as dog, vs the oracle's 56.2%), and reduced relearning ReAUC from 0.1909 down to 0.0580.

Lightweight Potato Leaf Disease Classification via Feature Distillation

July 2026 – Present
Supervisor: Dr. Pratik Mazumder (IIT Jodhpur) • PyTorch, torchvision, scikit-learn

Deploying plant disease classifiers on edge devices in the field requires extreme parameter efficiency without sacrificing robustness to uncontrolled lighting and background noise. Working on a 3,076-image uncontrolled-condition dataset across 7 disease classes, I designed a custom 238K-parameter CNN combining depthwise-separable convolutions with CBAM (Convolutional Block Attention Module) channel-spatial attention.

Representation Alignment: Rather than relying solely on output logit distillation (KL divergence), we aligned intermediate spatial feature maps from a fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 teacher (4.02M parameters) using a learned 1×1 convolutional projection head and cosine-similarity loss. Because the projection head is discarded after training, inference overhead remains strictly 238K parameters, 79.55% hold-out accuracy (0.791 macro F1) and retaining ~90% of the teacher's accuracy (88.8%) with 16.9× fewer parameters.

Selective Unlearning System: Hospital-Level Forgetting Exploration

Summer of RAID • 2026
RAID (AI & Data Science Society, IIT Jodhpur) • Literature & Preliminary Study

This summer project served as my introduction to machine unlearning. Our initial goal was to implement institution-level (hospital-level) unlearning on chest X-ray classifiers based on the ForgetMI paper, simulating a scenario where a contributing hospital revokes data access. While it proved challenging to simulate a multi hospital split using openly available datasets, tracing the citation network outward from ForgetMI led me to SCRUB (Kurmanji et al.), Bad Teacher Distillation (Chundawat et al.), and Selective Synaptic Dampening (Foster et al.). Realizing how easily surface-level unlearning methods could produce misleading accuracy drops directly inspired me to start working on my MNIST and CIFAR-10 audits.
